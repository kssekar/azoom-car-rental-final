How It Works

1. Reserve Online

Pick a car, branch and dates. Pay securely with your credit card.

2. Pick Up

Collect your electric car at the AZoom branch you selected.

3. Return

Drop it at a designated car park. Our staff inspect it for damages.

4. Final Bill

Pay the final bill (rental + any damages) by credit card.

Why electric? Zero tailpipe emissions, lower running costs, and a quieter drive. Join our environmental-conscious customers today.
